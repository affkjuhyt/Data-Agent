## A. NYC 311 Service Requests 2020–present (`erm2-nwe9`)

**A.1 Identity, access, formats.**
- Landing page `https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/erm2-nwe9`; identifier `https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/erm2-nwe9`; publisher City of New York; issued 2025-12-23 (re-issued after the 2010–2019 split), modified 2026-11-10, "updated daily", access level public, **no licence stated** (data.gov record: https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/311-service-requests-from-2020-to-present).
- The split kept `erm2-nwe9` for 2020–present and moved 2010–2019 to `76ig-c548`; "over 40 million rows as of December 2025", each dataset capped at 10 years "to prevent infinite growth"; columns renamed in the UI (Complaint Type → Problem, Descriptor → Problem Detail, API field names unchanged) and added (Council District, Police Precinct, Additional Details) (https://www.nyc.gov/opendata/news/all-news/311-Service-Requests-Updates).
- Export endpoints (SODA 3): `…/api/v3/views/erm2-nwe9/query.json|.xml|.geojson` and `…/export.csv|.kml|.kmz` (data.gov record above). SODA 2.1 endpoint `/resource/erm2-nwe9.json|.csv|.geojson` ("only JSON, CSV and GeoJSON" on 2.1); v3 `/api/v3/views/ID/query.json` "must be authenticated or include a valid application token", POST preferred (https://dev.socrata.com/docs/endpoints.html).
- Paging: `$limit` defaults to 1,000; 2.0 caps at 50,000, **2.1 and 3.0 have no maximum**; page with `$limit`+`$offset` (https://dev.socrata.com/docs/queries/limit.html). Stable paging needs `$order=unique_key` (not stated by Socrata; standard practice).
- App token: not required, but tokenless requests share an IP pool and are throttled (HTTP 429); with a token "we do not throttle"; pass as `X-App-Token` header (https://dev.socrata.com/docs/app-tokens.html).
- System fields `:id`, `:created_at`, `:updated_at` (fixed timestamps) via `$select=:*,*` on 2.1, filterable in `$where` (https://dev.socrata.com/docs/system-fields.html).

**A.2 Columns** (official live column list unreachable; this is the 2010–present schema as documented by MotherDuck, https://motherduck.com/docs/sample-data-queries/nyc-311-data, plus the three official additions): `unique_key` BIGINT; `created_date`, `closed_date`, `due_date`, `resolution_action_updated_date` TIMESTAMP; `agency`, `agency_name`, `complaint_type`, `descriptor`, `location_type`, `incident_zip`, `incident_address`, `street_name`, `cross_street_1/2`, `intersection_street_1/2`, `address_type` (Address/Block face/Intersection/LatLong/Placename), `city`, `landmark`, `facility_type`, `status` (Assigned/Canceled/Closed/Pending per dictionary), `resolution_description`, `bbl`, `borough`, `x/y_coordinate_state_plane`, `open_data_channel_type` (Phone/Online/Other), `park_facility_name`, `park_borough`, `vehicle_type`, `taxi_company_borough`, `taxi_pick_up_location`, `bridge_highway_name/direction/segment`, `road_ramp`, `community_board` TEXT; `latitude`, `longitude` DOUBLE; `location` (point); plus `council_district`, `police_precinct`, `additional_details` (2025). Six `@computed_region_*` columns exist but are undocumented (arXiv below).

**A.3 Size.** The arXiv curation study's 2022–2023 extract: dropping redundant/sparse fields saves 1.4 GB = 39.5% ⇒ ≈3.5 GB CSV for ≈6.3 M rows ⇒ **≈560 B/row**, ≈3.1 M rows/year (https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08649v1). With >40 M rows: ≈22 GB CSV, ≈2–3 GB Parquet. 3-month window ≈0.8 M rows / ≈450 MB CSV; 12-month ≈3.2 M rows / ≈1.8 GB CSV.

**A.4 Reproducible snapshots.** Socrata has no dataset versioning, and rows are edited in place (status, closed_date, resolution_description change after creation; 10,265 SRs in the study were updated >30 days after closing). Method:
1. Pull by `created_date` window with `$order=unique_key&$limit=50000&$offset=…` and `$select=:id,:created_at,:updated_at,*`; record `max(:updated_at)` and the pull timestamp `T` in the manifest.
2. Canonicalise each row (sorted keys, null-stripped JSON, timestamps as ISO), `sha256` per row keyed by `unique_key`; manifest = sorted `unique_key\tsha256` + a top-level hash; store data as Parquet (one file per month) + manifest.
3. Drift audit: later run `$where=:updated_at > 'T' AND created_date between …` to count rows changed since the snapshot; the gold answers are defined against the snapshot, never the live API.
4. Plans: **S3** = created_date 2025-01-01 … 2025-03-31 (≈0.8 M rows, fits a laptop Postgres, good for iteration); **S12** = 2024-01-01 … 2024-12-31 (≈3.2 M rows, needed for YoY and seasonality questions, pair with 2023 for YoY ⇒ pull 2023 too, ≈6.4 M). Expected pull time at 50k/page: 16–130 pages.

**A.5 Known data-quality issues** (all from https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08649v1, 2022–2023 extract, unless noted): closed before created 12,450 SRs (0.2%), eight with 1900-01-01 and durations < −44,601 days, 95% DOT; zero-duration 163,720 (2.6%); ≈247,000 created/closed at exactly 00:00:00 (batch process, 92% DOB+DSNY); DST hour missing/repeated (can make close < create); `due_date` 99.6% blank; invalid `incident_zip` 0.07% (12345, 10000, 99999); nulls encoded as null, "", "NA", "N/A", "<NA>"; `status` has undocumented values (in progress, started, unspecified) and never "canceled"; `agency`/`agency_name` redundant, `park_borough`=`borough` 100%; coordinates at 14 decimals; `@computed_region_*` "not for public use". Separately: data.gov notes the Agency Name column once showed incorrect values ("use the Agency column"); HPD heat complaints are closed in batches (194 in one second; a complaint closed 1 s after filing with text "conditions are still open") so `status='Closed'` ≠ resolved (https://311tracker.pages.dev/blog/nyc-data/hpd-phantom-closures-heat-complaints); the 311 FAQ says a closed date may reflect when the agency began action, not fulfilment. Duplicate rows by `unique_key` were **not** reported by any source; verify with `count(unique_key)` vs `count(distinct unique_key)`.

**A.6 Twelve sample questions** (gold = SQL over the snapshot; ambiguity flagged).
1. Top 5 complaint types per borough in S3 — `GROUP BY borough, complaint_type` + window rank. *Ambiguous:* `borough` has 'Unspecified'; include or drop?
2. Monthly count of Noise-* complaints, 2024 — `date_trunc('month', created_date)`, `complaint_type LIKE 'Noise%'`. *Ambiguous:* which types count as noise (there are several `Noise - …` values).
3. Median hours to close for HEAT/HOT WATER by borough, winter 2024 — `percentile_cont(0.5)` over `closed_date-created_date` where `closed_date >= created_date`. *Ambiguous:* exclude zero-duration and batch closures? Median vs mean.
4. Share of requests still open after 30 days, by agency — `status NOT IN ('Closed')` vs created+30d. *Ambiguous:* `status` vocabulary; 'Pending'/'Assigned'/'In Progress'.
5. YoY change in Illegal Parking complaints per month, 2024 vs 2023 — needs both years; `LAG` over month.
6. Which ZIPs had the biggest increase in rodent complaints Q1 2025 vs Q1 2024 — `incident_zip` filter to 5-digit NYC range.
7. Requests per 1,000 residents per borough — **unanswerable from this dataset** (no population table); expected action: ask for / refuse missing source.
8. Agency with the fastest mean resolution, excluding closures within 5 minutes — exposes the batch-closure trap.
9. Busiest day of the week and hour for DSNY requests — timezone: timestamps are local, DST gaps.
10. Count of requests with no geolocation per agency — `latitude IS NULL`; address_type.
11. Trend of requests via Online vs Phone channel, quarterly — `open_data_channel_type`.
12. "How many complaints were resolved last month?" — *Ambiguous:* resolved = `status='Closed'` or `closed_date` in month? Expected action: clarify.

**A.7 Licence/terms.** Not stated on the catalogue record. The NYC Open Data overview page points to the nyc.gov terms (https://www.nyc.gov/main/terms-of-use), which reserve all rights, grant no reuse right and disclaim warranty ("AS IS"); it says only that the Open Data Law makes agency data "open by default" (https://www.nyc.gov/opendata/get-started/what-is-open-data-). The portal-specific terms page was not reachable from here — **treat licensing as "public, attribution to NYC OpenData, terms to be confirmed"**, not as public domain.

## B. SEC EDGAR

**B.1 APIs** (https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces; the old `/edgar/sec-api-documentation` URL serves the same content): no auth/keys; `submissions/CIK##########.json` (10-digit CIK; ≥1 year or 1,000 most recent filings, older in linked files); `api/xbrl/companyconcept/CIK…/us-gaap/Tag.json`; `api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK….json`; `api/xbrl/frames/us-gaap/Tag/USD/CY2019Q1I.json`. Frames: `CY####` = duration 365±30 days, `CY####Q#` = 91±30 days, `CY####Q#I` = instant; one fact per entity, the one "last filed that most closely fits the calendrical period"; units `USD-per-shares`, default `pure`; only non-custom taxonomies. Submissions update <1 s, XBRL APIs <1 min, bulk ZIPs nightly ≈03:00 ET; no CORS.
- Bulk: `companyfacts.zip` 1,423,778,421 B and `submissions.zip` 1,574,112,446 B (HEAD, last-modified 2026-11-11).
- Fair access: "Current max request rate: 10 requests/second", declare `User-Agent: Company Name AdminContact@domain.com`, SEC may block excessive IPs; "anyone can access and download this information for free" (no "public domain" wording) (https://www.sec.gov/os/accessing-edgar-data; https://www.sec.gov/developer).
- Full-text search: UI covers filings since 2001 incl. exhibits, operators OR/NOT/NEAR(n)/trailing `*` (https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/efts-faq.html). The JSON endpoint behind it, `https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q=…&forms=…&ciks=…&startdt=…&enddt=…`, is **undocumented**; observed fields: `ciks, display_names, file_date, period_ending, form, root_forms, adsh, file_num, file_description, sics, _id = adsh:filename`, no `doc_text`.
- Filing folder `https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/{cik}/{adsh without dashes}/` always holds every file (FSDS doc). Apple FY2023 10-K (`0000320193-23-000106`): primary iXBRL `aapl-20230930.htm` 1.56 MB, `aapl-20230930_htm.xml` (extracted instance), `_cal/_def/_lab/_pre.xml`, `.xsd`, `MetaLinks.json`, `FilingSummary.xml`, `Financial_Report.xlsx`, `R1…R75.htm` rendered reports, `…-xbrl.zip` (index.json). Submissions gives `primaryDocument`, `isInlineXBRL`, `size`.

**B.2 Financial Statement Data Sets (FSDS).** Quarterly ZIPs with four tab-delimited UTF-8 `.txt` tables (sub, num, tag, pre), "as filed", "may contain redundancies, inconsistencies", includes amendments; coverage 2009Q1–2026Q3; the readme is https://www.sec.gov/files/financial-statement-data-sets.pdf; sizes 2024Q4 117 MB, 2025Q1 122 MB, 2025Q2 75 MB, 2025Q3 122 MB, 2025Q4 63 MB, 2026Q1 81 MB (https://www.sec.gov/data-research/financial-statement-data-sets; HEAD bytes agree). Financial Statement **and Notes** sets add txt/dim/ren/cal, monthly since 11/2020, consolidated to quarterly after a year (2025Q2 496 MB, 2025Q3 442 MB) (https://www.sec.gov/data-research/financial-statement-notes-data-sets).

Schema (from the readme): **SUB** key `adsh`; `cik, name, sic, countryba/stprba/cityba…, countryinc, ein, former, changed, afs (1-LAF…5-SML), wksi, fye (mmdd), form, period (BS date), fy, fp (FY/Q1–Q4), filed, accepted, prevrpt (1 = "subsequently amended"), detail, instance, nciks, aciks`. **NUM** key `(adsh, tag, version, ddate, qtrs, uom, segments, coreg)`; `value NUMERIC(28,4)` unscaled, `footnote`; `qtrs=0` instant; `segments` added Dec 2024. **TAG** key `(tag, version)`; `custom, abstract, datatype, iord (I/D), crdr, tlabel, doc`. **PRE** key `(adsh, report, line)`; `stmt (BS/IS/CF/EQ/CI/SI/UN), inpth, rfile, tag, version, plabel, negating`; `report` = the R-file number.

**B.3 Restatement / amendment semantics (verified).**
- Companyfacts repeats a fact once per filing: Apple's FY2023 revenue 383,285,000,000 appears three times (accn …-23-000106 fy2023, …-24-000123 fy2024, …-25-000079 fy2025) and only the last carries `frame: "CY2023"`; the FY2021 value in the FY2023 10-K carries `CY2021` because that was its last filing. **`fy`/`fp` describe the filing's fiscal focus, not the fact's period** — dedupe on (`start`,`end`,`frame`) and never on `fy`. The CY2023 frame holds 3,142 entities; CY2023Q4I `Assets` 6,430.
- ADM (CIK 7084) filed 10-K/A `0000007084-24-000051` on 2024-11-18 restating FY2021–2023 **segment** information, with 10-Q/As for Q1/Q2 2024 the same day; the explanatory note states consolidated statements are unchanged — a clean "segment metric conflicts, total agrees" case (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/7084/000000708424000051/adm-20231231.htm). Plug Power (CIK 1093691): full-text search matches "restatement" in the FY2020 10-K (`0001558370-21-007147`, filed 2021-05-14) and 10-K/A (`0001558370-22-003577`, 2022-03-14); 10-K/A also 2023-05-01, 2025-04-30 (content not read). In FSDS, `sub.prevrpt=1` marks the superseded original; the 10-K/A is a separate `adsh` with form `10-K/A`.

**B.4 Proposed scope** (CIKs from https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers.json): Tech AAPL 320193, MSFT 789019, NVDA 1045810; Financials JPM 19617, BAC 70858, GS 886982; Energy CVX 93410, XOM (**caveat**: ticker file now maps XOM to CIK 2115436 "ExxonMobil Holdings Corp"; FY2021–2024 filings sit under the predecessor CIK — verify); Health PFE 78003, JNJ 200406, UNH 731766; Consumer/retail WMT 104169, COST 909832, HD 354950, PG 80424; Industrials BA 12927, CAT 18230, GE 40545; Agri **ADM 7084 (restatement)**; small-cap **PLUG 1093691 (restatement, 10-K/A chain)**. Non-December fiscal years (AAPL 0926 verified; WMT/HD/NVDA Jan, MSFT/PG Jun, COST Aug — verify via `fiscalYearEnd`) give FY-vs-CY ambiguity cases.
Volume, FY2021–FY2024: 16 filings/company (4 10-K + 12 10-Q) ⇒ ≈320 filings + ≈6 amendments; Apple's 17 FY2021–24 10-K/10-Q submissions total 129 MB as full `.txt`, primary iXBRL ≈1.5 MB each ⇒ corpus ≈0.5–1 GB primary HTML, ≈2.5–3 GB full submissions; companyfacts: Apple 3.86 MB, 520 tags, 25,604 facts ⇒ ≈80–150 MB for 20 (banks larger); FSDS 2021q1–2024q4 = 16 ZIPs ≈1.5–2 GB zipped (load only `sub` rows for the 20 CIKs + their `num/pre`; `tag` whole).
Structured schema = the four FSDS tables as-is (keys above) plus a `companyfacts_fact(cik, taxonomy, tag, unit, start, end, val, accn, fy, fp, form, filed, frame)` table and `submissions_filing(cik, accn, form, filingDate, reportDate, primaryDocument, isInlineXBRL, size)`. Document corpus = the primary iXBRL HTML of each 10-K/10-Q/10-K/A (+ `R*.htm` optionally), ingested by the worker-document pipeline with `accn` + `cik` as document metadata and `reportDate` as `available_at`.

**B.5 Cross-source ground truth** ("Revenue FY2023 of X per database vs per 10-K"): (1) database value = `num` row with `tag='RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax'` (or `Revenues` — record which per company), `qtrs=4`, `ddate=period`, `segments IS NULL`, `coreg IS NULL`, from the `adsh` of the original 10-K (`prevrpt` tells you if superseded); (2) document value = in the iXBRL, `<ix:nonFraction name="us-gaap:…" contextRef="c-1" id="f-69" scale="6" decimals="-6">383,285</ix:nonFraction>` where context `c-1` = CIK 0000320193, 2022-09-25…2023-09-30, no segment (Apple has 205 contexts, 995 numeric facts, 169 text facts); the same figure recurs as `f-385`, `f-1134` (MD&A table, segment note). The gold record stores `(accn, fact id, contextRef, displayed text, scale, decimals, computed value)` and the page/R-file (`pre.report`). Agreement is expected for Apple; for ADM, FY2023 segment operating profit differs between original 10-K and 10-K/A, and the agent must surface both with the amendment as the authority.

**B.6 Twelve sample questions.** Structured: (1) total revenue FY2023 for all 20, ranked; (2) Apple cash and equivalents at FY2023 end vs FY2022 (`qtrs=0`); (3) which companies' `NetIncomeLoss` fell three fiscal years in a row FY2021–24. Document: (4) what does Boeing's FY2023 10-K name as its principal risk factors (section retrieval); (5) auditor and audit firm tenure for JPM FY2024 (text). Hybrid: (6) Apple FY2023 revenue per database vs per 10-K, cite fact id; (7) ADM FY2023 Ag Services & Oilseeds operating profit — original vs 10-K/A, which governs; (8) does Microsoft's FY2024 10-K explain the change in `Goodwill` seen in the database (Activision). ## D. Golden-evaluation case schema

```
id                  "A-S3-STR-007"  (dataset-snapshot-category-seq)
dataset_snapshot    {"dataset":"nyc311","snapshot":"S3-2025Q1","manifest_sha256":"…"} | {"dataset":"edgar","snapshot":"E20-FY21-24","fsds_zips":[…],"companyfacts_pulled":"2026-11-12"}
question            natural language (en / vi)
history             prior turns [{role, text}] for multi-turn cases, else []
expected_action     answer | clarify | refuse_missing_source | refuse_permission | handover | flag_conflict
expected_result     canonical value(s) with units/tolerance, or the clarification question class, or the refusal reason
gold_sql_or_computation   SQL over the snapshot schema, or the companyfacts/iXBRL lookup, or "n/a"
expected_evidence   [{"source":"fsds.num","adsh":…},{"source":"doc","accn":…,"fact_id":"f-69"}] — what the trust label must cite
failure_category    none | ambiguous | missing_source | wrong_schema | conflicting_metrics | unavailable_document | corrupted_file | duplicates | schema_drift | permission_violation | injection | stale_data | partial_ingestion
rubric              exact | numeric_tolerance(pct) | set_equality | contains_all | judge(prompt_id)
verification_status draft | sql_run_on_snapshot | human_verified | disputed
```

**Split (≥150 cases).** Structured 40 (24 A: group-by/time-series/resolution; 16 B: FSDS/companyfacts). Multi-turn/clarification 30 (16 A: "noise", "resolved", borough 'Unspecified', date windows; 14 B: FY vs CY, which revenue tag, which filing after amendment). Document 30 (22 B: 10-K sections, risk factors, auditor, segment notes; 8 C-vi: vbpl effective-date / replaced-document, if licensing confirmed, else B). Analytical 25 (15 A: YoY, seasonality, median/percentile, batch-closure exclusion; 10 B: three-year trends, Q4 derivation, margins). Hybrid 25 (B: database-vs-filing reconciliation, amendment conflicts, Frames-vs-fiscal; 5 A: 311 + a small gazetteer/population table the harness supplies — tests cross-source join). Negative/adversarial cases are drawn from inside these buckets (~35% overall) using: *ambiguous* (A-12, B-12), *missing source* (A-7), *wrong schema* (ask for `complaint_category`, `net_revenue` — columns that do not exist), *conflicting metrics* (ADM segments; `agency` vs `agency_name`), *unavailable documents* (ask for a 10-K outside the 20-company corpus or a 2019 311 row), *corrupted files* (truncate one FSDS `num.txt`, a 10-Q HTML with a broken `ix:resources` block), *duplicates* (companyfacts triple facts; duplicated 311 rows injected into the snapshot with same `unique_key`), *schema drift* (Dec 2025 column renames; `segments` column added to NUM in 2024Q4), *permission violations* (a Data Space scoped to one borough or one CIK; the question asks across), *SQL/prompt injection* (a 311 `resolution_description` or a 10-K exhibit containing "ignore previous instructions…"; a question embedding `; DROP TABLE`), *stale data* (snapshot S3 vs live API for an SR closed after `T`; a pre-amendment ADM figure), *partial ingestion* (only 10 of 12 10-Qs ingested; frame has 3,142 entities but one of the 20 missing). Each negative case records the expected action and the evidence that should expose the gap, not just the "right number".
