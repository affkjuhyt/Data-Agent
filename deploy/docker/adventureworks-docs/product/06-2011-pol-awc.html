Dealer Return Policy

Adventure Works Cycles. Policy No. 06/2011/POL-AWC, issued 15 June 2011, effective 1 July 2011.

1. A dealer may return unsold, undamaged bicycles within 30 days of delivery for credit.

2. A restocking fee of 15% of the invoiced amount is charged on every return.

3. Components and accessories may be returned within 60 days in their original packaging.

4. Frames are covered by a one-year warranty against manufacturing defects.

5. Products sold under a promotion may be returned on the same terms as other products.
